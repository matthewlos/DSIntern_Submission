# AI usage note

## How I used AI

I used AI to develop a clear description of the problem I chose and gain a deeper understanding of the data. I used Codex alongside Neovim to build `index.html` and complete the task.

## Where AI helped most

AI was most helpful in determining which rules to use when processing the data. After it identified the issues and explained what was incorrect, I was able to process the data correctly using:

- Deduplication by composite key
- Completeness detection
- Exclusion rules
- Parsing and normalization

This approach did not use a model or clustering.

## My decisions

I chose these rules myself rather than allowing AI to make the decisions for me, although I did use AI to implement them correctly.
